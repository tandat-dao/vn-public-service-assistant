      international adoption scope (e.g. Điều 29, Điều 40), not with adopter
      qualifications. The dense embedding vector for the full query lands closer
      to those "scope" articles than to Điều 14.

      dozens of the 75 chunks, producing near-flat BM25 scores (~0.029–0.032

      the dense stage already ranks Điều 29 first.

      khoản-split articles stored as 'Điều X Khoản Y' (prefixed). This does
